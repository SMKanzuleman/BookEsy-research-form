How it would work

One page for your clinic. Your patients do not need an account or an app.

Your first booking in a minute

  1. Choose a treatmentCheckup, cleaning, filling and more. You set the time and price.
  2. See free timesOnly open times are shown. A taken time disappears.
  3. Book and get a reminderThe patient gets a confirmation and a reminder before the visit.
